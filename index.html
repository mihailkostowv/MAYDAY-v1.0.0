ABOUT MAYDAY · VERSION 1.0.0

A growing record
of aviation history.

MAYDAY brings documented aviation accidents, terrorist attacks and combat losses onto an interactive globe. Its current scope is worldwide, from 1939 to 2026, across civil and military aviation.

Developed by Mihail Kostov. The archive is a work in progress; it does not yet represent complete historical coverage.

Markers use recorded coordinates, whose accuracy varies by source. Some locations identify a nearby place rather than an exact crash site. Events without usable coordinates remain searchable in the list. Source links let you investigate the original records.

Route lines connect a sourced departure or journey origin to the recorded event location. They are approximate geographic connections, not recorded flight tracks or flight altitudes. The line stops at the event location; an intended destination does not mean the aircraft reached it. Routes remain unavailable when the origin cannot be established.

Plane icons follow the approximate route arrival direction where one usable route exists. A reviewed heading takes priority. Route-derived direction is not an observed heading before impact; records without sufficient evidence retain a neutral orientation.

Globe: CesiumJS. Satellite imagery: Esri World Imagery and its credited contributors. Detail loads as you zoom; resolution varies by location. Red planes mark source-reported event locations.
